How it works

Six steps from symptom to fix.

IgnitionAI orchestrates the complete diagnostic workflow — turning every technician into a master diagnostician from day one.

Symptom Intake

Register vehicle and record customer-reported symptoms — rough idle, crank no-start, warning lights. IgnitionAI structures the context before any tool is connected.

OBD-II Connection

Connect any standard scanner to the DLC. IgnitionAI reads DTCs, live PIDs, and freeze-frame data — building a complete vehicle state snapshot automatically.

Causal AI Reasoning

Our causal physics engine reasons through vehicle subsystems — not keyword matching. Produces ranked root causes with probability scores in under 30 seconds.

Guided Diagnostic Tests

Step-by-step tests designed to confirm the root cause — coil swaps, fuel pressure checks, smoke tests. Systematic confirmation, zero guesswork.

Structural Repair Plan

Clear mechanical repair checklist with torque specs, Indian supplier part numbers, and estimated labour time — ready to hand straight to the technician.

Post-Repair Verification

Re-run diagnostics to confirm all ECU parameters are back in factory range. Generates a digital verification report — proof of quality against any comeback claim.
