Why This Matters Now

BS6 emissions standards have increased vehicle complexity, while the growth of independent and multi-brand service centers creates an urgent need for standardized diagnostic tools.

OBD Evolution

Three decades of diagnostic standards

1968

Volkswagen introduces first OBD scanning capability

1991

California mandates basic on-board diagnostics (OBD-I)

1996

OBD-II becomes standard across all vehicles

2025

IgnitionAI transforms diagnostic approach

Despite decades of evolution, current diagnostic tools remain code-centric rather than outcome-focused, creating opportunities for AI-driven solutions.
